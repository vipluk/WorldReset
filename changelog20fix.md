### 🐛 Fixed Bugs & Improvements in v2.0fix

* 🌌 **Limbo Void Fall Rescue Fix:**
  * Fixed an issue where players with average or poor internet connections falling into the void in Limbo would teleport to spawn but become frozen in place and invisible to other players.

* ⚡ **Safe Teleportation Engine:**
  * Modernized all teleportation actions across the plugin (entering/exiting Limbo, world resets, and backup restorations) into an asynchronous, lag-resilient pipeline.
