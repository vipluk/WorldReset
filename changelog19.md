# Changelog - WorldReset v1.9

### 🚀 What's New & Fixed in v1.9

* 🛡️ **Your GameRules Stay After Reset:** No more re-typing rules like `/gamerule keepInventory true` or `/gamerule doDaylightCycle false` every single time the world resets! WorldReset now automatically remembers all your active game rules and keeps them applied in every newly generated world.
* 💀 **Death Limit Fix:** Fixed an issue where turning off the death limit (`/wr death off`) or setting custom lives reset back to 1 life after a reset. Your death settings now save permanently.
* 🌍 **Consistent Difficulty in All Dimensions:** The Nether and The End now always match your Overworld's difficulty (e.g. Hard everywhere instead of resetting to Normal/Easy).
* 🍂 **Minecraft 26.3 Support & New Filters:**
  * Fully verified and working on the latest Minecraft & Purpur 26.3 update.
  * Added the new autumn biome `dappled_forest` and `pale_garden` to `/wr filter biome FORESTS`.
  * Added the new `ABANDONED_CAMP` campsite structure to `/wr filter structure`.
